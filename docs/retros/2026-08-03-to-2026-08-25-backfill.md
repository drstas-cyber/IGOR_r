## Methodology

Six categories, same scale as every prior article read this project has done: fabricated speech, misattributed quotes, prohibited claims, stats-vs-citations, identity block, quality/rendering.

- **Prohibited claims, identity block, stats-vs-citations, quality/rendering** — run through `tools/blog-generator/retroAudit.mjs` against all 17 live articles: `scanArticle()` (same generator demotion rules the pipeline already uses), `findIdentityCompletenessErrors()` (the new gate from item 1 of this hardening batch), a **live re-resolution of every citation URL** (not a replay of the generation-time result — a citation that resolved in July can rot by August), and `evaluatePublishStatus()` plus a live serve check.
- **Fabricated speech, misattributed quotes** — the two categories that need judgment, not a regex (`retroClaimGate.mjs`). **No `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` was available in this session**, so the automated LLM check could not run against all 17 — disclosed as its own finding on every article below, not silently skipped as clean. In its place: I personally read 3 articles in full for exactly these two categories, chosen for risk diversity (a citation-heavy legal-claims article, a neighborhood guide, and the one article the automated pass flagged for a real defect). The other 14 are not re-read here — see "Already individually read" below for why that's a documented decision, not a gap.
